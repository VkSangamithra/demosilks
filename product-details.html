Weaving & Technical Details

How To Preserve Your Heirloom

  • Dry Clean Only: Never machine wash or soak your pure silk handloom saree in water.
  • Muslin Wrap: Always wrap the saree in breathable pure cotton or unbleached muslin fabric (included complimentary with your order).
  • Air Circulation: Unfold and air your silk saree in shade every 4 to 6 months to prevent creasing along fold lines.
  • Zari Protection: Do not spray perfume, hairspray, or deodorants directly onto gold or silver zari threads.
  • Ironing: Always iron on the reverse side using medium-low heat or steam through a protective cotton cloth.
