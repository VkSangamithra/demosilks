Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need To Know

How does the WhatsApp Video Shopping consultation work?

Our senior saree stylist connects with you one-on-one via high-definition video call from our boutique. We drape each shortlisted saree on a dress form, show the border and pallu under natural daylight, crunch the silk to test purity, and display matching blouse fabrics.

Are all sarees 100% pure silk and certified with the Silk Mark?

Yes, absolutely. Every pure silk saree from DEMO SILKS carries the official Silk Mark Organization of India hologram tag. Our zari is tested grade-1 silver base electroplated in pure 24K gold, guaranteeing decades of lustre without tarnishing.

Do you provide custom bridal blouse designing & stitching?

Yes! Our master tailors and aari/zardozi embroiderers craft custom bridal blouses tailored to your measurement sheet. We also provide complimentary fall and edging (pico) service on all sarees upon request.

What are the international shipping timelines and charges?

We ship worldwide to the USA, UK, Canada, UAE, Singapore, Australia, and 40+ countries via insured DHL Express. Orders above ?15,000 enjoy free worldwide shipping. International transit time is typically 4 to 6 business days.
