Our Genesis

From Three Pit Looms to Royal Drapes

In 1984, as mechanized powerlooms began flooding markets with synthetic blends, third-generation weaver Devendra Sundaram gathered three master artisans in Kanchipuram under a sacred vow: Never compromise the truth of the thread.

Over 40 years, that courtyard atelier grew into an esteemed collective uniting weavers across Kanchipuram, Varanasi, Chanderi, and Arani—delivering heirloom authenticity directly to patrons.

Kanchipuram Roots Revived interlocking Korvai temple borders with 3 throw shuttles.
Zero Powerlooms Every single six-yard drape is woven purely by human hands on wooden looms.
Global Bridal Reach Over 25,000 brides adorned in USA, UK, Canada, UAE, and Singapore.
