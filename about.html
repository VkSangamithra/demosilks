01 / 03 Handloom Integrity

100% Handloom Integrity,
Zero Powerlooms

Every saree in our atelier is born on traditional pit looms that beat with the rhythm of Indian heritage. We work directly with over 450 hereditary weaver families across Kanchipuram, Varanasi, Chanderi, and Arani—eliminating middlemen and ensuring pure, ethically certified heirlooms.

Traditional Interlocking Korvai: Handcrafted with 3 throw shuttles by master weavers for seamless temple borders.
100% Govt. Silk Mark Certified: Pure natural Mulberry silk threads tested and certified zero synthetic blends.
Direct Fair-Trade Alliance: Fair living wages, health coverage, and pensions for 450+ generational artisan families.

02 / 03 Tested 24K Zari

Real Silver Core,
Dipped in 24K Gold

We strictly reject synthetic copper and plastic foils found in mass production. Our zari is spun from 98.5% pure silver thread, electrowashed in liquid 24 Karat gold, and accompanied by individual laboratory purity assay certificates for lifelong heirloom radiance.

Chemical Lab Assay Certified: Individual metallurgical purity assay certificate accompanying every bridal saree box.
Generational Non-Tarnishing Luster: Real silver core and pure gold bath preserves luminous sheen without blackening.
Sona-Rupa Kadwa Art: Delicate peacock, floral jaals, and meenakari motifs embossed without loose reverse floats.
